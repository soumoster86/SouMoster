## Introducing Bank Hopper

Following the successful global rollout of **Road Hopper v9.2.9**, SouMoster is thrilled to unveil our next major arcade project: **Bank Hopper**!

While Road Hopper had you dodging cars, trains, and rivers, **Bank Hopper** takes the adrenaline to an entirely new level with a high-stakes **bank vault heist** theme.

## The Heist Begins

In Bank Hopper, players put on a burglar mask and infiltrate high-security cyber financial depositories. Every hop tests your timing, reflexes, and nerves:

- **Infrared Laser Grids:** Time your leaps past pulsing, criss-crossing laser tripwires that activate and deactivate in rhythmic security cycles.
- **Pressure-Sensitive Alarm Tiles:** Watch your footing! Step on the wrong security sensor tile and trigger a lockdown alert.
- **Rotating Surveillance & Patrol Bots:** Slip outside the detection cones of automated guard bots and ceiling-mounted security cameras.
- **Cracking Electronic Vaults:** Hop your way to the inner sanctuary, crack digital security locks, and bag glittering gold bullion, stacks of cash, and legendary diamond relics.
- **Stealth Characters & Gadgets:** Unlock a crew of specialized heist hoppers, each equipped with custom gadgets, smoke bursts, and neon hop trails.

## Development Status

Bank Hopper is currently in active alpha development. We are focusing on tight, responsive swipe and tap controls, silky-smooth 60fps performance across budget and flagship Android devices, and offline-first gameplay so you can enjoy high-speed heists anywhere.

Stay tuned for upcoming sneak peeks, gameplay footage, and closed testing signups on Google Play!
