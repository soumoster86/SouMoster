## Introducing Bank Hopper

Following the successful global rollout of **Road Hopper v9.4.7**, SouMoster is thrilled to unveil our next major arcade project: **Bank Hopper**!

While Road Hopper challenged you to cross busy roads, rivers, and rails, **Bank Hopper** takes the signature intuitive hop controls into high-stakes **bank vault heist** action!

## The Heist Begins

In Bank Hopper, you control a thief hopping lane-by-lane out of a high-security bank after a major score. Every hop is a calculated decision:

- **One-Thumb Portrait Controls:** Tap forward to advance lane-by-lane, swipe left or right to evade guards and dodge laser tripwires, and hold to trigger your character's stealth ability.
- **Risk vs. Reward Greed Multiplier:** Do you bank your cash now at the safe extraction point, or push deeper into the vault for 2x, 3x, or 5x Greed Multipliers?
- **Escalating Security Meter:** The more cash you hold, the faster the security pursuit leash closes in. Keep your cool and outpace the alarm!
- **Gadgets & Power-Ups:** Collect game-changing pickups during your run, including Smoke screens, Speed surges, Shield bubbles, and Cash Magnets.
- **Buttery 60 FPS Native Performance:** Built natively in Kotlin with dedicated SurfaceView physics, responsive controls, and offline-first save support.

## Development Status

Bank Hopper is currently at **v0.7.4** in active preparation for closed testing on Google Play, with three complete environments (Vault, Security Floor, and Lobby), custom characters, and finalized Play Store release assets.

Stay tuned for closed beta access and pre-registration details!
