## NEW in 9.2.9

**Road Hopper is live on Google Play** — free for everyone.

### What's new (Play Store update)

- **Big performance update** — the game now draws on the GPU, so budget and older devices hold a steadier **60fps** where they used to stutter
- **UI fix** — NEW WORLDS / BIOMES title no longer overlaps the Back & Guides buttons
- **Less memory churn** mid-run

### What you can play

- **Adventure** — The Lost Litter: 9 chapters, stars, gifts and rescues
- **Endless** — a new random world every run, with weather and power-ups
- **Biomes** — lock to Meadow, Autumn, Desert, Tundra, or Jungle and hunt treasures
- **Worlds** — exclusive lands (Coast, Volcano, Night City, Storm Peak, Phantom Marsh, Orbital Deck)
- **Sprint** — race the clock for Velocity Tokens and Sprint heroes

Heroes, hop trails, daily missions, 60+ achievements, Google Play Games leaderboards — and **full offline play** (internet only for optional purchases).
