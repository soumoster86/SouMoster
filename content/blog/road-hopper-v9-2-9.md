## NEW in 9.2.9

**Road Hopper is now live on Google Play.**

- **Public release** — leave the beta invite behind; anyone can download and play
- **Launch-ready stability** — crash fixes and performance work carried forward from beta
- **Full arcade experience** — hop, dodge, collect, and climb your high scores
- **Characters & polish** — funny character names, hop sounds, Revive, and a cleaner launch screen

## From Beta to Live

Version **8.1.3** powered our open beta. With your feedback, we've shipped **v9.2.9** as the full Google Play release.
