## Introducing Space Hopper

With **Road Hopper v9.2.9** now live on Google Play, SouMoster is turning its focus to the next title: **Space Hopper**.

We're currently working on Space Hopper — a new hop-and-dodge experience set in space, carrying forward the same philosophy that shaped Road Hopper: simple controls, addictive runs, and polished feel.

## What to Expect

We're still early in development, but the goals are clear:

- **Familiar hop fun** — easy to start, hard to master
- **A new setting** — leave the highway and take the hop into space
- **Player-first design** — fair, polished, and fun to revisit

Details, screenshots, and a release window will follow as the game takes shape.

## Play Road Hopper Today

While we build Space Hopper, you can download and enjoy **Road Hopper** free on Google Play.
