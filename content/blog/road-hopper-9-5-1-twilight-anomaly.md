## The Twilight Anomaly

**Road Hopper v9.5.1** is rolling out on Google Play with our biggest Adventure update yet.

- **Campaign 4**: 9 new chapters and 72 marathon levels featuring volatile dual-hazard fusions
- **Boss Pursuits**: face the Chrono Shadow on every chapter finale
- **New Legendary Hero "Chrono"**: 20% faster hops, a starting Shield and Life, and hazard grace
- **Revive Safe Zone**: a shockwave clears ±2 lanes of traffic with 2 seconds of invulnerability
- **Complete English, Spanish and French localization**, plus performance fixes

[Get Road Hopper on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roadohopper.game)

Every game page now has a 30-second gameplay clip, captured from the games running on Android:
