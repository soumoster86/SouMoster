## Bank Hopper is live on Google Play

**Bank Hopper v1.0.0** is now available to everyone on Google Play. Hop lane-by-lane out of the bank with your cash, dodge laser grids and security drones, and decide when to bank your haul before the Security meter catches up. It's free, rated 7+, needs no account and plays offline.

Thank you to every tester who joined the Bank Hopper Closed Test. Your feedback shaped the onboarding, the leaderboards and the heist balance.

[Get Bank Hopper on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bankhopper)

## Time Hopper is in Closed Testing

**Time Hopper v1.13.2** is now on the Google Play Closed Testing track. Run through seven eras where every era has two worlds, Time Hop into the alternate timeline when the road closes in, and play the 21-level Chronicle campaign, Boss Gauntlet, Timeline Puzzles, a Daily Timeline with a ghost to race, and more.

To join:

1. Join the tester Google Group with your Google Play account: [groups.google.com/g/timehoppergame](https://groups.google.com/g/timehoppergame) (timehoppergame@googlegroups.com).
2. Open the [Time Hopper Play Store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soumster.timehopper) and become a tester.
3. Install the Closed Testing build from Play.

You can also request an invite on our [Beta page](/beta?game=time-hopper).

## Introducing Arena Legends: Gladiator Wars

We've started work on **Arena Legends**, SouMoster's first action game. You wake beneath the arena, and the pitmaster offers one bargain: fight, and earn your freedom.

- **A five-chapter campaign**: the Blood Pits, the Coliseum, the Wild Trials, the Ashen Arena and the Hall of Champions
- **Five bosses**, from Brutus the Iron Wall to the three-stance Imperial Champion
- **Real combat**: directional combos, charged heavy attacks, block, parry and counter, dodge, and breakable shields and armor
- **Four weapon classes**: Sword & Shield, Spear, Dual Blades and Great Axe, each with its own skill
- **Survival, Training and Boss Rush** modes

It plays in landscape with a joystick and action buttons. See screenshots on the [Arena Legends page](/apps/arena-legends), or [sign up](/beta?game=arena-legends) to hear first when testing opens.
