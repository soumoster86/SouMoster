## Arena Legends is live on Google Play

**Arena Legends: Gladiator Wars v1.0.2**, our first action game, is now available on Google Play. Fight through five campaign chapters, from the Blood Pits to the Hall of Champions, and face five bosses with four weapon styles. Build weapon mastery to unlock helmet crests, shield designs, heraldic standards and metal finishes. Take on Survival with perks, Boss Rush, the Daily Challenge and the rotating Weekly Champion. It's free to play, and core gameplay works offline.

Thank you to everyone who joined the Arena Legends Closed Test. Your feedback shaped the controls, the pacing and the final release.

[Get Arena Legends on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.arenalegends.game)

## Nitro Racer is in Closed Testing

**Nitro Racer**, our first racing game, is now on the Google Play Closed Testing track. The latest builds add new scenery to all 32 circuits, from wildflower meadows and coastal dunes to snowy passes and volcanic roads. Full-screen race paintings now appear while your next race loads, and a successful gold purchase gets a coin-and-confetti celebration.

To join, submit the Google Play email from your Android phone on our [Beta page](/beta?game=nitro-racer). We add you to the tester list, and you receive a link to opt in and install from Google Play.

## Introducing Lantern Depths

We've started work on **Lantern Depths**, a turn-based roguelite where your lantern's oil is your health, your light and your ability fuel, all at once. Every hit you take dims the flame, so the weaker you get, the less you can see.

- **Every step is a decision**: nothing moves until you act, and lit enemies show their next move
- **Lantern actions**: Flare to stun light-sensitive foes, Shut to sneak, Burn barrels and Kindle fires
- **Short runs, long hooks**: pick a relic for each descent, and spend embers at Wellhead Camp to unlock Wicks, the Warden and the Archive
- **One-thumb portrait play**, with a left-handed HUD, Fast mode and reduced flicker

See screenshots on the [Lantern Depths page](/apps/lantern-depths), or [sign up](/beta?game=lantern-depths) to hear first when testing opens.
