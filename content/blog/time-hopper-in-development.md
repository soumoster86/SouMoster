## Introducing Time Hopper

SouMoster is working on a new Hopper adventure: **Time Hopper**. It carries forward the series' simple controls, addictive runs, and polished feel. We are early in development, so details, screenshots, and a release window will follow as the game takes shape.

## Bank Hopper: heading to production

**Bank Hopper v0.8.0** has been in Google Play Closed Testing, and we're now ready to **apply for production access**. Thank you to every tester who joined the [tester Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/bank-hopper-game) and sent feedback. Once Google approves the application, Bank Hopper can launch publicly worldwide.

## Road Hopper v9.4.9

**Road Hopper** is live on Google Play at **v9.4.9**, with continued performance and stability polish.
