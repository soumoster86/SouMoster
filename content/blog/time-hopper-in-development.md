## Introducing Time Hopper

SouMoster's next game is **Time Hopper**: *Hop Through Time. Break History. Fix the Timeline.*

It's a portrait three-lane runner, built natively in Kotlin, in which a traveller with a damaged time device sprints through seven eras: **Prehistoric, Ancient Kingdom, Medieval, Industrial Age, Modern City, Cyber Future** and the **Chrono Void**.

### The Time Hop

The signature move is the **Time Hop**. Spend 35 energy and you slip into an alternate version of the timeline for three seconds. The rock in front of you might vanish, or a laser barrier might be waiting instead. Purple echoes show what's on the other side, so the real skill is reading them and deciding whether to dodge or hop.

### Paradox, pursuits and the big decision

- **Paradox** rises every time you bend history. Push it too far and echoes drift, hazards exist in both timelines, and a **Chrono Guardian** comes for you.
- **Every era has a pursuit**: a T-Rex, a collapsing temple, siege fire, a runaway train, a crumbling highway, hunter drones and a time storm. Hopping is how you escape.
- **At every Time Gate you choose**: extract and bank your relics, or push deeper into time for bigger rewards.

### What's already in the build

Time Hopper is a playable development build (**v1.1.3**) with seven characters (Max Tempo, Dr. Nova, Rex, Lady Epoch, Glitch, Captain Clock and Zero), six gadgets, six modes (Classic, Daily Timeline, Endless, Paradox, Relic Hunt and Hardcore), a relic museum, daily and weekly missions, and an original soundtrack. It plays fully offline.

Next comes playtesting on real devices to tune pursuit pressure and difficulty. See the [Time Hopper page](/apps/time-hopper) for more, or [sign up](/beta?game=time-hopper) to hear first when testing opens.
