## Road Hopper v9.6.0

Four mechanical **New Worlds** join Road Hopper:

- **Clockwork Quarry**: open bridges with gear plates and dodge minecarts
- **Willow Span**: time the rising bridges, or catch a boat
- **Swing Ferry**: ride moving ferries through a cargo harbor
- **Tip Deck**: balance tilting platforms and dodge steam vents

There are also two new Adventures, each with 9 chapters and 72 uniquely named levels. **The Crossing Works** tours all four worlds, while **Frontier Relay** mixes them with five classic biomes. That brings Road Hopper to six Adventure campaigns.

Version 9.5.2 also added an optional **Simplified Menu** that puts all five modes in one picker. Switch layouts any time in Settings → Visuals.

## Nitro Racer v0.5.4 (Closed Testing)

Nitro Racer's closed test has grown a lot since our last update:

- **12 championships and 48 circuits**, with autumn roads, misty highlands, tropical islands and a moonlit spaceport, plus new landmarks and regional lighting on every circuit
- **Rush Hour**: weave through hatchbacks, taxis, vans and motorcycles on six circuits, cross checkpoints to add time and chain close overtakes into multipliers up to x5
- **Racing Journey**: driver XP and levels, car mastery for all 10 cars, a rotating three-race Weekly Cup and an Explorer Album of hidden tokens on every circuit
- **Guided practice** for every control, larger touch buttons and SouMoster Games billboards trackside

Join the [tester Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/roadhopper) with the Google account on your phone, then opt in and install from the [Nitro Racer page](/apps/nitro-racer).
