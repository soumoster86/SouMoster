## Closed Testing is open

**Bank Hopper v0.8.0** is now on the **Google Play Closed Testing** track. Invited testers can install the heist hopper from the Play Store, send feedback, and help us polish the game before a worldwide public launch.

Road Hopper remains live on Google Play. Space Hopper continues in development.

## What testers can play

Closed Testing 0.8.0 includes:

- **Tap to bank** — booths light up as you near them; lock cash in or run past
- **Full Heist** — all eight floors in one run as security rises
- **Global Play Games leaderboards** — Best Score, Full Heist, Fastest Escape, Lifetime Loot
- Characters including **Rookie, Grabber, Insurance, and Black Limo**
- Bag cosmetics, Pickup and Crew Van getaways, and themed menus
- A shorter start: two coached runs, with the rest taught in play

## How to join

1. Join the official tester Google Group: [groups.google.com/g/bank-hopper-game](https://groups.google.com/g/bank-hopper-game).
2. Open the [Bank Hopper Play Store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bankhopper) and tap **Become a tester**.
3. Install the Closed Testing build from Play.

You can also request an invite on the [Closed Testing signup page](/beta?game=bank-hopper).
