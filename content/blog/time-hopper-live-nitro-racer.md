## Time Hopper is live on Google Play

**Time Hopper v1.15.1** is now available to everyone on Google Play. Run through seven eras where every era has two worlds, Time Hop into the echo timeline when the road closes in, and cross the new **Timeline Bridges** that only exist in one timeline. Play the 21-level Chronicle campaign, race your ghost in the Daily Timeline, and personalise your traveller with outfits, trails, gates and Time Hop effects, including the new **Emerald Expedition** collection. It's free, rated 3+, and core gameplay works offline.

Thank you to everyone who joined the Time Hopper Closed Test.

[Get Time Hopper on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.soumster.timehopper)

## Arena Legends is in Closed Testing

**Arena Legends: Gladiator Wars v0.1.11**, our first action game, is now on the Google Play Closed Testing track. Fight through five chapters and five bosses with four weapon styles, take on Survival, Boss Rush and the Daily Challenge, and earn 20 achievements. Move and resize the action buttons, play left-handed, and keep optional cloud backups with Google Play Games.

To join, submit the Google Play email from your Android phone on our [Beta page](/beta?game=arena-legends). We add you to the tester list, and you receive a link to opt in and install from Google Play.

## Introducing Nitro Racer

We've started work on **Nitro Racer**, SouMoster's first racing game. Steer through sweeping bends, slipstream your rivals and unleash nitro on the road to the chequered flag.

- **8 championships and 32 circuits**: countryside, coastal roads, alpine passes, neon city nights, desert canyons, rainforest, arctic and volcanic roads, plus mirrored variants
- **Five AI rivals**, nitro bottles, gold trails, boost pads, shortcuts and overtake combos
- **A garage to build**: six cars with engine, turbo, tyre and nitro upgrades, plus paint, stripes, underglow and number plates
- **Time trials** against your saved ghost, daily challenges and a driver profile of your own
- **Tilt or on-screen steering**, and offline racing with no account needed

See screenshots on the [Nitro Racer page](/apps/nitro-racer), or [sign up](/beta?game=nitro-racer) to hear first when testing opens.
